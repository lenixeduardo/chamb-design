Perguntas frequentes

Is Charm-Design really free?

The entire platform is MIT licensed and self-hostable. Hosted collaboration is the only paid part, and you can always run it yourself instead.

Which AI models can I use?

Any of them. Claude, GPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, OpenRouter, plus fully local Ollama and LM Studio. Providers are plugins, so adding one is a small file.

What does the exported code look like?

Idiomatic components with Tailwind classes and design tokens — no proprietary runtime, no wrapper divs, nothing you would be embarrassed to commit.

Can I keep my data on my own machine?

Yes. Point the provider at a local model, use the local storage adapter, and no part of your project leaves your network.
